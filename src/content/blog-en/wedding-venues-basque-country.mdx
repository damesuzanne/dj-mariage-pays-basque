The French Basque Country has no shortage of places to get married: châteaux beside a lake, Basque farmhouses, hillside estates and palace hotels facing the ocean. Here are 14 wedding venues, from the coast around Biarritz to the inland villages, with what their official websites say about their history, their contact details and a direct link to find out more.

This selection is neither a ranking nor a complete list. Phone numbers are given in international format, for couples calling from abroad.

## On the coast and nearby

### Domaine de Bassilour, Bidart

The estate’s farmhouse dates back to 1562 and the Manor, built in 1870, was extended in 1900. The Vachet family has owned it since 1989. Today, the Manor’s grounds host the ceremony and the drinks reception, the renovated Farmhouse hosts the reception, and guest rooms mean you can stay on site.

1562 rue Bassilour, 64210 Bidart · [+33 5 59 41 90 85](tel:+33559419085) · [Official website](https://www.domainedebassilour.com/) · [History of the estate](https://www.domainedebassilour.com/en/our-rooms/the-history-of-domaine-de-bassilour/)

### Brindos, Lac & Château, Anglet

Ten minutes from Biarritz, the Château de Brindos stands on the shore of a peaceful lake. Weddings here enjoy an intimate setting, between the water, the countryside and the château’s indoor spaces.

1 allée du Château, 64600 Anglet · [+33 5 59 51 53 63](tel:+33559515363) · [Official website](https://brindos-cotebasque.fr/) · [Weddings and receptions](https://brindos-cotebasque.fr/fr/page/mariages-receptions-brindos-lac-chateau-anglet-cote-basque.29592.html)

### Domaine de Gaztelur, Arcangues

The former manor house dates from 1401. Its name means “the house on the water” in Basque. Javier de la Rica imagined it as a refuge for his family and friends, filling it with objects from all over the world, and his daughter Marta, an interior designer, redesigned the layout of the rooms. The estate includes a restaurant, a florist and 3 hectares of grounds, and hosts weddings on request.

Chemin de Gastelhur, 64200 Arcangues · [+33 5 59 23 04 06](tel:+33559230406) · [Official website](https://gaztelur.com/) · [The story of the place](https://gaztelur.com/projets/)

### Grand Hôtel Spa & Thalasso, Saint-Jean-de-Luz

The Grand Hôtel grew out of the Hôtel Terminus, built in the dunes at the end of the 19th century, destroyed by fire in 1904 and rebuilt on a larger scale as the Modern Hôtel. It took its current name in 1982. Facing the bay, it now hosts weddings and receptions in a Belle Époque setting.

43 boulevard Thiers, 64500 Saint-Jean-de-Luz · [+33 5 59 26 35 36](tel:+33559263536) · [Official website](https://luzgrandhotel.fr/) · [History of the hotel](https://luzgrandhotel.fr/en/discover-the-grand-hotel/)

### Hôtel du Palais, Biarritz

The Villa Eugénie, which became the Hôtel du Palais, was built in 1855 by Napoleon III for his wife, Eugénie de Montijo. The Salon Impérial, the Emperor’s former ballroom, and its Winter Garden are among the spaces offered for weddings.

1 avenue de l’Impératrice, 64200 Biarritz · [+33 5 59 41 12 34](tel:+33559411234) · [Official website](https://www.hyatt.com/unbound-collection/fr-FR/biqub-hotel-du-palais-biarritz) · [Weddings](https://www.hyatt.com/unbound-collection/fr-FR/biqub-hotel-du-palais-biarritz/weddings)

### Château du Clair de Lune, Biarritz

Hidden in eight hectares of century-old parkland, this 19th-century mansion is a few minutes from the beaches. The covered reception room, the château terrace and the French formal gardens host the wedding, and a restaurant occupies an 18th-century Basque farmhouse on the property. Hosting a wedding means booking out the hotel for several nights, to be confirmed for your date.

48 avenue Alan Seeger, 64200 Biarritz · [+33 5 59 41 53 20](tel:+33559415320) · [Official website](https://www.hotelclairlune.fr/) · [Weddings and events](https://www.hotelclairlune.fr/mariages-evenements/)

## Estates and villages inland

### Domaine de Larbéou, Bayonne

This Anglo-Norman style residence was built in 1876. Bordered to the south by the Adour river, the property covers seven hectares and includes the Manor House, the 500 m² Great Hall and guest accommodation. Only one event is held per day, so the venue is yours alone.

12 chemin d’Estebot, 64100 Bayonne · [+33 5 59 55 32 80](tel:+33559553280) · [Official website](https://www.larbeou.com/) · [The estate](https://www.larbeou.com/le-domaine)

### Domaine Santa Maria, Villefranque

The story began in 1997 on the hill that gives the venue its name, with Papi Michel, a builder, and his son Jimmy. After ten years of weddings and family dinners, a fire destroyed everything and the family chose to start again from scratch, inspired by their travels. The new estate offers spaces for 20 to 850 people, including a patio, grounds and a sheltered terrace.

Chemin de Quartier Bas, chemin Santa Maria, 64990 Villefranque · [+33 6 79 04 37 16](tel:+33679043716) · [Official website](https://www.domainesantamaria.com/) · [History of the estate](https://www.domainesantamaria.com/domaine)

### Domaine de Larraldia, Villefranque

The château was built in the 17th century by the knight Garat, whose daughter married Adam de Larralde. In 1813, Napoleon’s army and the Duke of Wellington’s forces clashed here. Acquired in 1822 by Louis Miguel d’Arcangues, it was sold by the family in 1926 and became a luxury hotel after the war before being abandoned. In 2019, Sylvie and Bertrand rediscovered it under the vegetation and restored it between 2020 and 2023. Its 300 m² orangery now hosts the evening celebrations.

404 chemin de Larraldea, 64990 Villefranque · [accueil@chateaudelarraldia.fr](mailto:accueil@chateaudelarraldia.fr) · [Official website](https://www.chateaudelarraldia.fr/) · [The château’s history](https://www.chateaudelarraldia.fr/domaine)

### Maison Belzunce, Méharin

The Jauregia de Belzunce, a 14th-century manor house, was home to the Navarrese Belzunce family for nearly three centuries. The former sheepfold has become the great hall, which seats up to 200 guests, with lounges, gardens and a swimming pool. Visits are by appointment.

467 chemin d’Etxegorria, 64120 Méharin · [+33 7 78 92 36 65](tel:+33778923665) · [Official website](https://maisonbelzunce.com/) · [Wedding receptions](https://maisonbelzunce.com/reception-de-mariage-64/)

### Les Collines Iduki, La Bastide-Clairence

According to its website, the village is listed among the “most beautiful villages in France”. The town hall, a Basque church, the residence with its three wedding halls, its restaurant and the Basque houses where guests can stay are all within 300 metres, and a wedding planner helps with the organisation.

Route de la Côte, 64240 La Bastide-Clairence · [+33 6 38 70 80 83](tel:+33638708083) · [Official website](https://www.mariage-pays-basque.fr/) · [The halls](https://www.mariage-pays-basque.fr/les-salles/)

### Auberge Ostapé, Bidarray

High above Bidarray, the private estate covers 45 hectares of hillside around a 17th-century farmhouse and red-tiled houses where the guest rooms are. A celebrant-led ceremony in the meadows, drinks by the pool and a brunch at the inn are all possible.

1135 Xahatoeneko bidea, domaine Chahatoenia, 64780 Bidarray · [+33 5 59 37 91 91](tel:+33559379191) · [Official website](https://www.ostape.com/fr/) · [Private receptions](https://www.ostape.com/fr/receptions-privees/)

### Ferme Inharria, Saint-Pée-sur-Nivelle

On the Ibarron square, opposite the pelota wall dated 1824, the Inharria farmhouse is described by its website as having stood there for more than 400 years. Converted into a reception venue, it has kept its Basque folk-art decoration. Bookings are made through the Hôtel Bonnet.

6151 chemin d’Ibarrondoa, quartier Ibarron, 64310 Saint-Pée-sur-Nivelle · [+33 5 59 54 10 26](tel:+33559541026) · [Official website](https://ferme-inharria.com/)

### Urtubien Borda, Urrugne (Olhette)

This 17th-century Basque farmhouse prides itself on being “on Napoleon’s road since 1670”. It has a reception hall, a garden, a terrace and five guest rooms, close to Saint-Jean-de-Luz.

290 chemin Inzola, Olhette, 64122 Urrugne · [+33 6 11 73 35 70](tel:+33611733570) · [Official website](https://www.urtubienborda.fr/)

## How to choose between these 14 venues

Start with two or three criteria: the number of guests, whether you need to stay on site and the atmosphere you want, from the seaside to the hills or a château. Then visit the venues that remain with the same list of questions, and compare quotes for the same scope. If you are planning from abroad, a video tour or a trip to see two or three shortlisted venues in one weekend saves a lot of time.

The information in this article comes from the venues’ official websites, consulted in October 2026. Availability, prices and terms should be confirmed directly with each venue.
